# Friendship, tension and the late acknowledgment

Version 1.4 · Current story direction · Planned, unbuilt and untested

Alice and Daniel stay friends throughout their lived past. Attraction can be visible before either names it, but they do not date, kiss, share a lease or mutually confess at eighteen, twenty-one or twenty-two. Their first mutual acknowledgment occurs in C7S2, after Alice has learned the encounter's limits and expressed her preference about repeated activation, before the common goodbye and all three ending choices. It is a shared authored event. No reply, puzzle result or ending earns it.

The [story bible](01-story-bible.md) and current screenplay own the events and dialogue. This document tracks why the player should care about each scene and what changes; it is not a competing screenplay or a new runtime system. All twelve interactions, two Alice-viewpoint scenes, 28 shared slots and three ending meanings remain within the existing scope.

## 1. What keeps the friendship alive

Their bond has value before its romantic possibility is spoken: they make things, share bad food, take each other seriously, enjoy being ridiculous and know how to help. Attraction changes the cost of asking for time or admitting fear; it does not explain every kindness. Alice's work with Mara and her own decisions remain necessary parts of the story.

The stages must differ. Childhood builds ease and shared habits. At eighteen, choosing more time together becomes noticeable. At twenty-one, assumed availability and delayed disclosure injure trust. At twenty-two, they repair how they make plans while keeping separate homes and ambitions. The final encounter lets them name something they did not manage to name in life. It does not convert their actual friendship into years of failure.

Avoid a chain of almost-confessions interrupted by buses, phones or convenient visitors. Each withheld statement needs a character reason and a changed consequence. C3S1's stay/walk choice expresses company; C4S3's booking exposes an assumption; C4S4's visit plan changes behavior. None is a romance test. By C7S2, the acknowledgment can be direct because the uncertainty has changed, not because Daniel has completed enough tasks.

## 2. Tension has several forms

A live question sustains attention: *Can I make this work? Will she say what she wants? Will he accept the correction? What can this recording really do? What should happen after success?* These questions do not all require danger. Comfort, pride and laughter can finish a scene honestly while a practical consequence or changed understanding carries interest forward.

Keep two levels legible. The immediate task gives the player something understandable to want now. The larger question concerns what Daniel is doing with his life and what these people mean to each other. Do not make every immediate task carry the whole tragedy. P03 can simply be satisfying creation; the fundraiser can really succeed. The loss will matter more because those pleasures were substantial.

The tables describe intended causes of interest, not measured player responses. “Next” may point to a later return of a question across the authored time cuts; it does not add a quest, required click, timer or collectible.

## 4. Keep, cut and stage

Keep the imperfect food, a working radio, Alice's odd sound film, the fundraiser win, Ruth's retirement joke and Jonah's competent help. These scenes establish what is worth caring about. Do not score them as a countdown toward Alice's death or add symptoms that imply an avoidable outcome.

Cut repeated explanations once the action demonstrates the distinction. Do not ask whether Alice will come home in several different forms after her work decision is settled. Let the later question concern whether Daniel can remain a good friend without deciding her future. Replace generic yearning with an observable action: stay, help, ask, return property, keep a commitment or make room for a refusal.

Keep the three C7S2 empty shots fully empty and silent of people and score. The mutual acknowledgment happens before Turn around, in the existing encounter composition, followed by the silent views and common goodbye. The receiver's success is not fraudulent; it genuinely makes this conversation possible. Equally, Alice's feelings are not a fee paid for Daniel's withdrawal from life. Her preference against repeated activation remains intact after affection is expressed.

E2's existing hand contact remains a consequence of failing boundaries. It gains no first confession, kiss, exclusive proof of love or guarantee of another life. E1 cannot import the encounter's new words into the fixed summer. E3 does not retract the acknowledgment or replace Alice with a new romance. All three inherit the same truth and differ in what Daniel does with it.

The 151–153-minute allocation is unmeasured. Adding the late exchange does not authorize extra held shots or a longer finale: trim repeated reassurance and explanatory lines within the existing beats, then measure a read. If the scene needs more time after that, revise the estimate explicitly. Do not compress the three empty views to make room mechanically.

## 5. What to test next

Use the bounded [friendship and tension table read](13-paper-playtest-pack.md) with current canonical scenes. Ask readers to describe the relationship in their own words, name earlier behavior that supports the late exchange, explain Alice's independent plans, and identify where a scene stopped changing. Avoid asking whether a scene feels “epic enough.”

Pay special attention to three possible failures: friendship feeling like stalled dating; repeated hesitation becoming a device rather than behavior; or the late acknowledgment reading as a reward for obsession or bodily death. Fix a concrete line, gesture or scene order before adding another subplot. Preserve reports that a reader simply preferred the friendship without romantic acknowledgment; that is different from failing to understand the intended ending.

No reader responses, playtest results, new implementation estimates or claims of sustained excitement exist yet. This revision makes the intended tension concrete enough to review and test.
